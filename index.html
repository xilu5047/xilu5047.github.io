01 / RESEARCH

Research interests.

I study when and how latent causal structure can be identified from observed distributions, especially when measurements arise across environments with different generating mechanisms.

CURRENT RESEARCH DIRECTION

Causal Representation Learning from Multiple Distributions

Variation across distributions can reveal structural information that may be inaccessible from a single environment. I am interested in how cross-environment changes constrain learned latent representations, and under what assumptions the underlying Markov or moral graph can be recovered.

Multi-environment distributionsLatent graph recoveryIdentifiability theory
R / 01

Latent structural identifiability

Identifying graph structure even when a latent representation itself is not fully determined.

R / 02

Changes across environments

Understanding which types of environmental variation provide sufficient information for recovery.

R / 03

Learning under missing observations

Studying the effect of incomplete or informative observations on identifiability across distributions.

Research interests and ongoing questions are described here; they are not presented as published results.
